Privacy Policy for CueFlow

Effective date: 5 October 2026

CueFlow is an Android teleprompter application. This policy explains how CueFlow handles information when you use the app.

Scripts, imports, settings and backups

Your CueFlow scripts, app settings and text extracted from documents are processed on your device for the app's teleprompter features. CueFlow does not upload your script text or imported document content.

When you choose a document through Android's system file picker, the selected storage provider may be local or a cloud provider you chose. Workspace backups and exported scripts are ordinary files saved to the location you select. Keep exported files private if they contain sensitive content.

Advertising and payments

The current CueFlow release is ad-free. It contains no advertising SDK, advertising consent SDK, in-app purchase, or payment SDK. CueFlow does not display advertisements and does not use your script or app activity for advertising.

Floating teleprompter permission

CueFlow can request Android's Display over other apps permission so a user-started floating teleprompter can remain visible above another app. CueFlow uses this permission to display and control its own floating reader. It does not use the overlay permission to read or control the content of the app underneath it.

On supported Android versions, CueFlow also uses a foreground-service notification while the floating reader is active. Notification permission may be requested so those controls can be shown.

Feedback email

If you tap Send feedback by email, CueFlow opens an installed email application using a mailto: action addressed to pdnexus.apps@gmail.com. The draft includes CueFlow's app version, your device model and Android version to help diagnose issues, plus space for your feedback. CueFlow does not send the message automatically. Nothing is sent unless you choose to send it from your email app.

Accounts and cloud sync

This version of CueFlow does not require a CueFlow account and does not provide CueFlow cloud sync for scripts.

Retention and deletion

Local CueFlow data remains on your device until you delete it, clear app storage or uninstall the app. Files you exported remain wherever you saved them. Your chosen email or storage provider handles information you send to it under its own policy.

Children

CueFlow is not designed specifically for children under 13.

Security

No method of electronic storage is completely risk-free, so users should avoid putting unnecessary sensitive information in feedback messages or unprotected exported files.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when CueFlow's features, SDKs or legal requirements change. The effective date above will be updated when a revised policy is published.

Contact

For privacy questions or support, contact pdnexus.apps@gmail.com.
