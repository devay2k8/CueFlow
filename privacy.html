Privacy Policy for CueFlow

Effective date: 12 October 2026

CueFlow is an on-device teleprompter application available for Android and Windows. This policy explains how the current CueFlow editions handle information.

Information CueFlow does not collect

CueFlow does not require an account and does not include analytics, advertising, advertising-consent, tracking, cloud-sync, or payment SDKs. CueFlow does not sell personal information and does not use scripts, document contents, settings, or app activity for advertising.

Scripts, imports, settings and backups

Your scripts, settings, and text extracted from imported documents are processed and stored locally on your device for CueFlow's teleprompter features. CueFlow does not upload script text or imported document contents to a CueFlow server.

When you choose a document through an Android or Windows system file picker, the storage provider may be local or a cloud provider you selected. Workspace backups and exported scripts are ordinary files saved to the location you choose. Those providers and locations operate under their own privacy and security terms.

Network access and external applications

The current CueFlow app interface and reader use bundled local content. When you choose to open the privacy policy or send feedback, CueFlow hands that request to your browser or email application. Your browser, email provider, operating system, or chosen storage provider may process information under its own privacy policy.

Floating teleprompter

On Android, CueFlow can request the Display over other apps permission so a user-started floating teleprompter can remain visible above another app. CueFlow uses this permission only to display and control its own floating reader; it does not use the permission to read or control the content underneath it.

On supported Android versions, CueFlow uses a foreground-service notification while the floating reader is active. Notification permission may be requested so those controls can be shown.

On Windows, the floating reader is a user-controlled, always-on-top window. CueFlow requests exclusion from compatible Windows screen-capture methods. CueFlow does not capture the screen or read content from other applications.

Feedback and support email

If you select Email Feedback & Support, CueFlow opens your installed email application with a draft addressed to pdnexus.apps@gmail.com. The draft includes space for your feedback, CueFlow's application version, relevant device or operating-system details, and a text-only signature.

You can review, edit, or discard the draft. CueFlow does not send it automatically. Nothing is sent unless you choose to send the message. Avoid including scripts, confidential information, or unnecessary personal information in feedback.

Data retention and deletion

Local CueFlow data remains on your device until you delete it, clear the app's storage, remove the relevant local files, or uninstall the app. Files you exported remain wherever you saved them and must be deleted separately.

Security

CueFlow limits its active features to the permissions and local data required for the teleprompter experience. No method of electronic storage is completely risk-free, so keep devices, exported backups, and feedback messages appropriately protected.

Children

CueFlow is not designed specifically for children under 13 and does not knowingly operate a service that collects children's personal information.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when CueFlow's features, platforms, or legal requirements change. The effective date above will be updated when a revised policy is published.

Contact

For privacy questions, feedback, or support, contact pdnexus.apps@gmail.com.
