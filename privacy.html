Privacy Policy for CueFlow

Effective date: 5 October 2026

CueFlow is an Android teleprompter application. This policy explains how CueFlow handles information when you use the app.

Scripts, imports, settings and backups

Your CueFlow scripts, app settings and text extracted from documents are processed on your device for the app's teleprompter features. CueFlow does not upload your script text or imported document content to its advertising or purchase-verification services.

When you choose a document through Android's system file picker, the selected storage provider may be local or a cloud provider you chose. Workspace backups and exported scripts are ordinary files saved to the location you select. Keep exported files private if they contain sensitive content.

Floating teleprompter permission

CueFlow can request Android's Display over other apps permission so a user-started floating teleprompter can remain visible above another app. CueFlow uses this permission to display and control its own floating reader. It does not use the overlay permission to read or control the content of the app underneath it.

On supported Android versions, CueFlow also uses a foreground-service notification while the floating reader is active. Notification permission may be requested so those controls can be shown.

Advertising

The Google Play version of CueFlow may display a banner advertisement using Google Mobile Ads (AdMob). Google's advertising SDK may process information such as IP address (which can be used to infer approximate location), device or advertising identifiers, app interactions and diagnostic information for advertising, analytics, fraud prevention, security and compliance purposes.

Where required, CueFlow uses Google's User Messaging Platform to request consent and provide privacy choices before eligible advertising requests. Ad privacy choices are available from the app when required by the consent SDK.

Remove Ads purchase

CueFlow may offer a one-time Google Play purchase that removes advertisements. Google Play processes the payment. CueFlow does not receive your full payment-card details or Google password.

To verify the purchase and restore the ad-free entitlement, the Play-distributed build can send the Google Play package name, product ID, purchase token, a random CueFlow installation identifier and a one-time nonce to CueFlow's purchase-verification service over HTTPS. The service does not receive your scripts or imported documents. Purchase tokens are treated as sensitive credentials; the verification design stores a token hash for the purchase record and stores a raw token only in encrypted form when an acknowledgement retry is necessary.

When Google Play and the verification service confirm that the one-time product is owned and acknowledged, CueFlow suppresses and destroys its normal ad banner. Pending, cancelled, failed or unverified purchases do not grant the ad-free entitlement.

Feedback email

If you tap Send feedback by email, CueFlow opens an installed email application using a mailto: action addressed to puneety2k25@gmail.com. The draft includes CueFlow's app version, your device model and Android version to help diagnose issues, plus space for your feedback. CueFlow does not send the message automatically. Nothing is sent unless you choose to send it from your email app.

Accounts and cloud sync

This version of CueFlow does not require a CueFlow account and does not provide CueFlow cloud sync for scripts.

Retention and deletion

Local CueFlow data remains on your device until you delete it, clear app storage or uninstall the app. Files you exported remain wherever you saved them. Purchase-verification records may be retained as needed to maintain and validate the one-time entitlement, prevent abuse, handle refunds/revocations and meet operational or legal requirements. Google and your chosen email or storage provider handle data under their own policies.

Children

CueFlow is not designed specifically for children under 13.

Security

CueFlow uses HTTPS for configured purchase-verification traffic. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely risk-free, so users should avoid putting unnecessary sensitive information in feedback messages or unprotected exported files.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when CueFlow's features, SDKs or legal requirements change. The effective date above will be updated when a revised policy is published.

Contact

For privacy questions or support, contact puneety2k25@gmail.com.
