# PD Nexus Branding Policy

_Last updated: 12 October 2026_

This policy applies to all existing and future PD Nexus applications, including Android APK and Windows editions.

## 1. No logo implementation

- Do not add any PD Nexus logo, symbol, or graphic.
- Do not modify existing application icons.
- Do not replace individual app branding.
- Do not introduce registered trademark symbols.
- Do not create new PD Nexus logo assets.
- Preserve every application's existing name, icon, and identity.

## 2. Approved text-only branding

Use exactly:

**Envisioned by PD Nexus**

**IDEAS • APPS • IMPACT**

Support: [pdnexus.apps@gmail.com](mailto:pdnexus.apps@gmail.com)

Keep the exact spelling, capitalization, tagline, and support address.

## 3. Required locations

### Splash or loading screen

Show the small text **Envisioned by PD Nexus** where appropriate without increasing startup delay.

### Settings → About

Display:

- Creator credit
- Tagline
- Application version
- Support email

### Settings → Feedback & Support

Provide a complete, functional, review-before-send feedback route addressed to [pdnexus.apps@gmail.com](mailto:pdnexus.apps@gmail.com).

The draft may include relevant application version and device information for support, but nothing may be sent until the user reviews and sends it.

### Email signatures

Use text-only branding:

**Envisioned by PD Nexus**  
**IDEAS • APPS • IMPACT**  
Support: [pdnexus.apps@gmail.com](mailto:pdnexus.apps@gmail.com)

## 4. Strict implementation rules

- Preserve all existing app names, icons, identities, features, workflows, and data structures.
- Do not alter unrelated features.
- Do not change backend functionality without explicit approval.
- Follow each application's privacy, security, feedback, testing, and no-regression requirements.
- Do not implement a PD Nexus logo unless explicitly authorized by a later instruction.

This policy supersedes all earlier PD Nexus logo-related requirements.
